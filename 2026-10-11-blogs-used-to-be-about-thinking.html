Blogs used to be about thinking

2026-10-11

I am starting this blog because I miss the kind of blog that was just someone thinking out loud. Somewhere along the way a lot of blogs became tutorials and documentation, which are useful, but they leave out the part where you get stuck and change your mind.

Vlogs are a good comparison. A vlog is basically a blog in video form, and nobody expects one to be a manual. It’s just someone recording their journey, which is closer to what blogs were supposed to be.

So this is my attempt at that: short notes on what I’m building and learning, mistakes included. It’s also, honestly, a project to show how I work, so I wrote the whole thing from scratch in plain Python and HTML, with no frameworks.
